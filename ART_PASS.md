## D1 — THE ROOM (not a slate void)
Current state: flat `#171a21` ColorRect behind floating dark panels. The game
plays in a void; nothing sells "shop".
Direction: every DAY plays inside a fully dressed 2D room backdrop that sits
in a CanvasLayer (layer = -1) behind all UI. TWO themed worlds ship:

[a] CORNER SHOP AM — morning corner shop, warm daylight.
  Backdrop wall: assets/art_pass/corner_shop_am/tex_wallpaper.png (tileable,
  cream base 214,196,160 with faded stripe + hairline cracks).
  Furniture band: honey-oak counter along the bottom third
  (tex_counterwood.png tiled horizontally) — THE COUNTER IS THE PAYOFF
  SURFACE: the shelf the player stocks reads as standing on/behind it.
  Wall dressing: 2 shelf rails with pastel tin rows + hanging brass pan
  scale + awning valance strip (props_plate.png cutouts, placed per the
  mood board composition).
  Mood words: warm daylight, honey oak, pastel tins, dust motes.
  Palette anchors: cream (214,196,160), oak (166,118,66), pastel pink/green/
  yellow tins, espresso ink (90,70,50).

[b] NIGHT MARKET PM — lantern-lit stall after dark.
  Backdrop wall: assets/art_pass/night_market_pm/tex_plaster.png (deep teal
  36,66,72 with damp blooms + cracks), falling to near-black at edges.
  Furniture band: red stall cloth with gold diamond stitch along the bottom
  third (tex_stallcloth.png tiled) — same payoff-surface role as the AM
  counter.
  Wall dressing: paper-lantern cluster (emissive warm cores 250,196,96),
  chalkboard menu sign, orange crate + fairy lights (props_plate.png).
  Wet-floor reflection strip under the stall band (dark, broken verticals
  of lantern color).
  Mood words: lantern ember on teal, red silk, neon reflections.
  Palette anchors: teal (36,66,72), crimson (158,44,52), ember (250,196,96),
  gold stitch (212,168,92), orange fruit (226,130,44).

ROOM <-> DAY MAPPING (locked): day % 2 == 1 -> CORNER SHOP AM, else NIGHT
MARKET PM (Day1 AM, Day2 PM, Day3 AM, Day4 PM, Day5 AM). One constant
(`FINAL_DAY_PM := false`) lets engineering opt Day 5 into the night market
finale later WITHOUT another design pass. Room crossfades (0.6s) on NEXT DAY.

## D2 — THE LIGHT (one dramatic key, hard falloff)
Shadow-form's single SpotLight3D translates to 2D as painted light, not real
lights (gl_compatibility canvas, zero perf cost):
- CORNER SHOP AM: one diagonal window-light pool from upper-left across the
  wall — a soft-edged warm quad (Color(1.00,0.94,0.80) at alpha ~0.16,
  blurred edge ~120px) reaching the counter. Right edge of screen falls to
  shade (multiply gradient to Color(0.82,0.74,0.62)). Floating dust motes:
  ~14 slow-drifting 2-3px warm specks inside the light pool only (CPUParticles2D
  or a tiny shader), alpha <= 0.35.
- NIGHT MARKET PM: three radial ember pools hanging from the top edge
  (lantern positions per mood board): core (250,196,96) alpha 0.30 blending
  out over radius ~220px each; everything outside pools multiplies down
  toward (0.10,0.14,0.18). Lantern cores themselves get a 1-frame-free
  gentle flicker (alpha noise ±8%, ~2Hz, never synced).
- BOTH ROOMS: ambient dimming layer sits ABOVE the backdrop but BELOW game
  panels; panels themselves stay fully readable (contrast is non-negotiable —
  badge text and card tags keep current ink colors).

UI material policy:
- Category colors KEEP their hue identity (readability is gameplay); they are

  as lit by the room, not pasted on.
- Panels stop being dead flat: shelf/tray panels adopt a faint texture wash —
  StyleBoxFlat bg blended 12% toward the room's furniture texture average
  (AM oak, PM crimson), border color follows room line color
  (AM 39404f -> 4a3a28 warm; PM 39404f -> 27444b teal).
- Rule badges remain the dominant band (binding park condition stands);
  their pill backgrounds take the room wash at 20%, verdict colors unchanged
  (GOOD 3ddc84 / BAD ff5c74).

## D4 — THE PAYOFF FLASH (OPEN SHOP moment)
The reveal equivalent of shadow-form's lock-chime flash. On a passing audit:
1. Warm flash: full-rect ColorRect (1.00,0.95,0.85), alpha tween
   0 -> 0.55 in 0.06s, ease-out decay to 0 over 0.45s.
2. Radial glow bloom from the shelf band center (sprite, additive, scale
   0.6 -> 1.15 over 0.35s, alpha 0.45 -> 0).
3. Badges tick in sequence (40ms stagger left->right) with their existing
   chime; the LAST badge lands as the flash peaks.
4. Vignette breathes IN with the flash (strength 0.42 -> 0.52, back over 0.6s).
On a failing audit: red vignette pulse instead (BAD tint, alpha 0.25,
0.3s), spoil_buzz unchanged.
Persistent post layer (CanvasLayer above UI, mouse_filter IGNORE):
vignette_strength 0.42, vignette_radius 0.72; film grain amount 0.05,
grain_size 1.7px, re-seeded every frame (canvas_item shader — gl_compatibility
safe). These numbers mirror the custodian-accepted shadow-form D6 table.

## D5 — THE AUDIO (per-room ambient arrangement)
Existing SFX set stays (place_thock, badge_chime, shop_bell, spoil_buzz,
hint_ping — provenance already in assets/assets.json). Arrangement changes:
- CORNER SHOP AM: shop_loop.ogg continues as the bed (-16 dB, unchanged).
  Add gentle room EQ character: highpass 90Hz, lowpass 9kHz (bus effect) —
  reads as "small bright interior".
- NIGHT MARKET PM: darker bed — either (preferred) a night_market_loop.ogg
  produced by the MUSIC lane to the spec below, or (fallback) shop_loop at
  -3 st pitch with a low-shelf +3dB @ 200Hz and slower LFO'd volume (+-1.5dB,
  0.15Hz) reading as night air. Spec for music lane: ~7-9s seamless loop,
  70-80 BPM feel, low drone + cricket-tick percussion + distant chatter
  texture, mono 22kHz OGG, <= 400KB.
- Room-scoped SFX tuning (no new mechanics): place_thock plays -2dB brighter
  (+1.5kHz shelf) in AM, warmer (low shelf +2dB @ 300Hz) in PM; shop_bell
  full-bright in AM, half-bright + 80ms longer decay in PM.
- Ducking doctrine (audio-design skill): the payoff flash ducks the ambient
  bed -6 dB for 0.5s while the bell rings (sidechain-style volume automation),
  release 300ms. Master keeps >= 6dB headroom; limiter on Master as safety.

## D6 — FEEL (co-sign REQUIRED before engineering implements)
Target feel, beat by beat — this is the section the gate signs:
- BOOT (AM days): you walk into a warm little shop before opening. Calm,
  papery, sunlit. Not cozy-cutesy: tidy, grown-up, a bit nostalgic.
- BOOT (PM days): the same shop at night — intimate, ember-lit, the day's
  last restock. Slight mystery without horror.
- PLACING AN ITEM: a confident wooden THOCK onto real wood/cloth, not a
  click into a spreadsheet. Cards should feel like tins and parcels.
- VIOLATION: a sour buzz + the room itself seems to flinch (red vignette
  pulse). Feedback is spatial, not just textual.
- OPEN SHOP (THE PAYOFF): the whole point. Flash + bell + sequential green
  ticks = a small ceremony. Should land like a receipt stamp + cash-drawer
  chime. Players should want to hear it again.
- NEXT DAY: 0.6s crossfade between rooms — the shop lives one long day;
  AM/PM alternation is the clock you can feel.
Co-sign question for the gate: does this feel direction deepen "tidy shop,
quiet pride" WITHOUT touching rules/difficulty? Verdict requested: SIGN /
SIGN-WITH-NOTES / REJECT.
